# Changelog

## 3.3.0 (candidate)
- Fewer worlds per page after the tax office test (27.11.): one gradient world that suits the accent plus an optional Night band, tiles and cards off-white, never photo tiles and gradient tiles in one row. Lint warns when a page uses more than two gradient worlds. Services without a real moment become a typographic price list instead of empty tiles. Visible structure: hairlines between list entries, light grey containers for sections that belong together.
- Gradients recalibrated on Amelie's reference screenshots after a fourth round: dusky mid-tone worlds with depth (amber, lilac, rose, dusty sky, straw, night), visible grain, one real-moment card per tile. Lint warns about neon stops, washed-out surfaces and look-alike gradients. Icons named for services, steps, cards and contact.
- Gradients rebuilt after three rounds on a driving-school page: neutral page, two to four soft grain surfaces, each its own hue world and light direction; calibrated mesh set in gradients.md. Lint now warns about gradient stops that are too strong and about surfaces that mix the same hues, instead of a single-family hue limit.
- House rules from Amelie's approved list (27.11.): house-rules.md with rule and why; Albert Sans, Hanken Grotesk or Manrope in light to regular weights, no AI faces; off-white #F9FAFB cards without border, shadow or glow; no pill buttons; subtle gradients with fine grain on the background only; Hugeicons thin, no Lucide; images as placeholders (library pool or calm panel with a photo note), never AI images. Lint now flags AI faces, heavy headlines, any shadow, pill buttons and Lucide imports.
- Against the AI look: reference hunt (Framer Marketplace and others) and a one-sentence concept with one signature moment before any layout; AI-tells list; no two neighbouring sections share a skeleton; one oversized typographic moment; offline parts are explicitly not a page template.
- Works fully without Systra Tools: new offline recipe service-site.md (hero with a real moment, services, process, statement, contact), router column for the offline route, image order without the library. Tested on a dental practice page without any MCP call or library image.
- Brand read in step 1 sets one gradient family and an intensity (Calm, Medium, Vivid); taste-lint reports hue spread, dashes, serif faces, kickers, borders, heavy shadows and AI copy patterns.
- Adapting checklist for library source, image search with filters, recipes for whole pages.
- Finish checks: truthful live data, nowrap for prices and numbers, mobile reading order, placeholder people.
- Calibration file with two worked briefs (painter, hair salon).
- Taste first: new always-read taste bar with hard rejections, a 9-of-10 screenshot score, alternative rules, service-business section roles, image prompt structure and copy rules.
- Systra signature made concrete: Albert Sans alone by default, white foundation with one brand accent, no serif accent, no mono kickers, no beige or olive house palette.
- MCP first: search the connected Systra Tools MCP with job plus industry; local recipes and components become re-themable fallbacks.
- Bundled components and recipes re-themed to neutral white, near-black and one accent token; eyebrow labels are optional.
